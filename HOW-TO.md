# Sending an email to lots of your customers

This tool sends **one separate email to each person** from your Outlook address. Everyone sees only their own name, never anyone else's. Every email shows up in **Sent Items** like normal, and replies come back to your inbox.

## First time only
1. Open the MailMerge web address you were given. It asks for a password.
2. Click **Accounts > Add account**, give it a name, click **Save**, then **Sign in**.
3. Click the blue link, type the code shown, sign in with your shop email, click **Accept**.

## Every time
1. **Contacts:** click **Upload Excel / CSV** and choose your customer spreadsheet. The top row should be headings (like *First Name*, *Email*). Check the preview, choose which column is the email, and click **Import**. You can fix names, add people, or tick *Don't email* right in the table. New customers later? Upload again into the same list and only new people are added.
2. **Emails > New email:** pick the list, write the subject and message. Click a column name (like *First Name*) to put it in, so each person gets “Dear Sarah” instead of a generic hello. Add attachments if you like.
3. **Check it:** click **Preview** to see the email for a real customer, then **Send me a test** and look at it in your inbox.
4. Then choose:
   - **Create drafts:** nothing is sent. A draft for every person appears in your Outlook **Drafts** folder so you can read and change them first.
   - **Send:** sends them one by one, slowly (about 20 a minute). You can **Pause** any time and carry on later.

## Good to know
- **Don't close anything or worry:** it keeps going on its own. If a message says **Check**, look in Sent Items, then press *Mark sent* or *Retry*.
- **Someone asks to stop getting emails?** Add them under **Opt-outs**. They'll be skipped from then on, in every email.
- **Only email people who have agreed to hear from you.** New Zealand law requires that for promotional email, plus your shop's details and a way to say no. A line at the bottom like “Don't want these? Just reply STOP” does the job.
- Start with a small batch (10–20) the first time. Email from a shop mailbox shouldn't be a huge blast.
- Your usual Outlook signature isn't added automatically. Paste it at the bottom of the message.
